How to read a hypothesis

Question: What mechanism or combination is worth investigating?

Evidence: What observations support it?

Challenge: What evidence argues against it?

Uncertainty: What do we still not know?

Test: What experiment could support or falsify it?

Lifecycle & revision

A hypothesis is not treated as permanently true. New evidence can change its ranking or state. Published objects can become SUPERSEDED or WITHDRAWN while preserving their history and successor bindings.
