Search public research hypotheses

This interface loads the generated hypothesis index, then reads each canonical hypothesis.json object. Filters are projections of those objects; this page does not maintain a second scientific database.

Research boundary: results are research hypotheses for investigation, not medical advice, treatment recommendations, dosing instructions or claims of patient benefit. Protected evidence-relationship review state is loaded from the governed exception register; a registered disagreement is unresolved scientific state, not permission for autonomous normalisation or accepted evidence.

Disease context is research-object metadata, not patient-specific clinical information. Missing or unresolved context remains absent rather than inferred.

Protected-review filtering derives only from the governed evidence-relationship review-exception register. A registered exception records an unresolved interpretation and does not decide which relationship is correct.

Date filters use the canonical hypothesis provenance update date. They do not imply that literature searches are complete through that date.
